Four studio disciplines

Full-scope 3D production for architects, property developers, game teams, and product brands worldwide.

Architectural visualization

01

Exterior and interior stills, daylight and blue-hour atmospheres, camera tours, and dimensioned floor plans from CAD or design briefs.

  • Cycles AgX stills and architectural camera shift alignment
  • Measured PNG floor plans and bilingual material boards
  • Daylight and dusk lighting scenarios for client presentations

Interactive WebGL walkthroughs

02

Real-time browser 3D tours built directly from architectural plans so buyers and stakeholders explore spaces on desktop or mobile.

  • Three.js WebGL scenes with baked lighting and room presets
  • Direct shareable URL requiring zero app downloads
  • Optimized GLB delivery tuned for mobile and laptop GPUs

Environments and worldbuilding

03

Large-scale terrain, foliage kits, and real-time worlds engineered for Unreal Engine 5.8 World Partition and film production.

  • Hero vegetation and props with LOD0 to LOD3 and UCX hulls
  • 16-bit 1 m heightmaps, water masks, and scatter placement tables
  • Verified standalone frame budgets and clean engine packaging

Custom 3D pipelines and assets

04

Modular furniture catalogs, calibrated PBR texture sets, Blender 5.2 scripting, and strict QA verification before engine handoff.

  • Real-world metre scale with clean naming and collection layout
  • Automated geometry, UV, normal, and re-import QA checks
  • Multi-format delivery in Blender, Unreal Engine, FBX, and GLB
